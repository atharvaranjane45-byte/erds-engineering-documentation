# Citation and reference rules

1. Follow the institution's required citation style when given.
2. Otherwise choose a consistent style suitable for the document. IEEE style is often a sensible default for engineering, but it is not universally required.
3. Cite sourced facts, specifications, figures, equations, claims, and paraphrased research.
4. Place citations close to the supported claim and include a complete reference list.
5. Cite original source pages rather than search-result snippets whenever accessible.
6. For figures adapted from external sources, cite the source in the caption and check reuse rights.
7. For generated conceptual diagrams, label them as created for the report; do not imply that they were sourced or experimentally validated.
8. Do not invent references, DOI values, page numbers, authors, or access dates.
9. Check that every in-text citation has a matching reference and that references are actually cited.
10. Clearly mark unavailable or unverified source details rather than silently guessing.
